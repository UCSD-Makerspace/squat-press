# SOURCE — Mouse Water-Reward Dosing (literature)

> Immutable extract. Cited literature on water-reward volumes and water
> restriction for head-fixed / operant mouse behaviour (retrieved 2026-07).

## Key figures
- **Per-reward volume:** canonical ≈ **8 µL/trial** (typical range 4–10 µL).
- **Daily total (welfare-critical):** water-restricted mice should receive **≥ 1.0 mL/day**; common working target **~1.0–1.5 mL/day**.
- Body weight held at **~80 % of baseline**, monitored daily. Mice tolerate water restriction less well than rats — IACUC-approved protocol + daily weighing mandatory.

## Applied to this rig
- Protocol: **150 lifts/day**, **5–6 lifts per dose** → **~27 doses/day**.
- Target daily ~1.2 mL ÷ 27 ≈ **~44 µL/dose** (≈ 8 µL/lift — matches canonical).
- Top up to the 1.0 mL minimum if the animal earns less; adjust per-dose volume to hold ~80 % baseline weight.
